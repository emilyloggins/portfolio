date: 2026-10-19
question: Light mode, dark mode, or both?
considered:
  - Light first, dark designed as a second palette
  - Dark first, the school's default
  - One palette, no toggle
decided: One palette. The ground is a chosen colour, not white or black, and everything is measured against it once.
mine: true
evidence: []
---
"Light mode and dark mode are sort of antiquated trends." A site is one material, the way a printed object is. No `prefers-color-scheme` branch, no second palette to maintain, and the ground becomes the first real design decision instead of a default.
