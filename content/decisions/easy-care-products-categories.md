The user needs to know that every option they want is going to be there, and they need to see that everything the patient's coverage requires is included. Structures that compute coverage and rebuild the list around it duplicate products and hide the shelf the clinic admin set. Clinic favorites are the clinic's own categories. So the rows are those categories, each product once, the requirement stated once. Coverage is a lookup from the catalog, and it informs rather than disables: a vet may want a combo plus a separate dewormer for whipworm. The clinician decides; the doctor verifies at sign-off.
