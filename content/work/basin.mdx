I have struggled with my skin my whole life. Years of frantically adding and removing products, then learning the hard way that the only way through was to strip it all down to basics and slowly add things back in, watching. I started taking a picture every day, but there was no way to carefully compare, and keeping track of the dates, what I was testing, and what I thought was laborsome. One day I was taking a picture and thought how nice it would be to line up my face with past pictures and slide to compare. That started the whole idea.
