A year of Easy Care means a year of healthy living for a pet. Instead of one big vet bill, pets can get access to all their services and preventive care for a recurring monthly cost. Every clinic can fully customize their offerings, and products ship right to the pet’s door.

## Enroll a pet

<Cart />

Following a wellness visit, enrollment happens in one conversation: during an exam, over a counter, or in the waiting room. Enrolling a pet is a process quick enough to fit into any clinic user’s workflow, while clear enough to provide accurate coverage for each unique pet.

<Screen src="work/easy-care/02-what-new-style.png" alt="Easy Care's What step: choosing Cricket's plan, preventive products and add-ons, with the enrollment cart beside the form" aspect="2480 / 2516" />

## Sign the prescriptions

<Screen src="work/easy-care/10-queue-new-style.png" alt="Easy Care's prescription queue: six pets waiting for a doctor's signature, with their client, products, plan and how long each has waited" aspect="2480 / 1598" />

Once a pet is enrolled in a plan, the plan gets sent to the assigned doctor. This is where they review and sign the prescriptions, making modifications when necessary.

<Sign />

When a signed prescription is submitted, an order is created at the pharmacy. Shortly after, the pet will receive their first dose in the mail.

## Monitor the plan

<Nav />

Easy Care provides the tools for clinics to maintain each plan throughout the year. Users can see which services a pet has used, follow orders from the pharmacy, and catch any snags early before they become a problem.

<Screen src="work/easy-care/20-client-profile-new-style.png" alt="Easy Care client profile for Dillon Johnson: Cricket’s Small Dog Plan with services used so far, products shipped, and the plan’s billing, renewal and actions" aspect="2480 / 2607" />

Every night, Easy Care syncs with the clinic’s PIMS software so that all redeemed services are up to date on the pet’s page. The clinic can come here to see how much has been utilized, and make adjustments.
