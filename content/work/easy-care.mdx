A year of Easy Care means a year of healthy living for a pet. Instead of one big vet bill, pets can get access to all their services and preventive care for a recurring monthly cost. Every clinic can fully customize their offerings, and products ship right to the pet’s door.

## Enroll a pet

<Cart />

Following a wellness visit, enrollment happens in one conversation: during an exam, over a counter, or in the waiting room. Enrolling a pet is a process quick enough to fit into any clinic user’s workflow, while clear enough to provide accurate coverage for each unique pet.

<Screen src="work/easy-care/02-what-new-style.png" alt="Easy Care's What step: choosing Cricket's plan, preventive products and add-ons, with the enrollment cart beside the form" aspect="2480 / 2464" />

## Sign the prescriptions

<Screen src="work/easy-care/10-queue-new-style.png" alt="Easy Care's prescription queue: six pets waiting for a doctor's signature, with their client, products, plan and how long each has waited" aspect="2480 / 1550" />

Once a pet is enrolled in a plan, the plan gets sent to the assigned doctor. This is where they review and sign the prescriptions, making modifications when necessary.

<Sign />

When a signed prescription is submitted, an order is created at the pharmacy. Shortly after, the pet will receive their first dose in the mail.

## Monitor the plan

<Nav />

Easy Care lets clinics maintain each plan throughout the year. They can see which services a pet’s used, follow the orders from the pharmacy, and catch any snags early before they become a problem.
