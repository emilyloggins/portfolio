Wellness-plan software for veterinary clinics, built with MWI.

- Role: product designer and front-end developer
- Where: Greenline, in partnership with MWI
- Stack: HeroUI, Figma variables, an MWI theme on top of the kit

Easy Care lets a clinic enroll a pet in a yearly wellness plan and ship its preventive products from MWI. The hard problem: making this tool flexible enough to be used by anyone in the clinic at any time in the patient's experience or clinic's workflow, while providing enough guardrails to design an effective, safe medical plan for the next year of a pet's life.
