Easy Care is how a vet clinic sells a year of care in one conversation. A receptionist or tech sets a pet up at the front desk, a doctor signs the prescriptions between patients, and the preventives ship to the pet parent each month. A monthly plan replaces the big annual bill, and the clinic gets what a plan is really for: a pet that shows up all year.

## The system

Built on HeroUI's kit, themed to MWI by repointing fourteen tokens, and extended only where the kit had nothing.

<Screen src="work/easy-care/system-theme.png" alt="Tokens: the MWI colour ramp, the fourteen aliases and what each colour means. Two kit variants and four new components cover all five flows. Colour carries meaning: purple navigates, green is a plan running as intended, amber is paid but blocked on a person, red changes the system and always confirms." />
